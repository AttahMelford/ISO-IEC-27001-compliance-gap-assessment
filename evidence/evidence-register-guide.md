# Evidence Register Guide

## Purpose

This guide explains how evidence will be tracked during the simulated ISO 27001:2022 compliance assessment.

## Evidence Collection Process

1. Identify the ISO 27001 control being assessed.
2. Determine what evidence is needed to evaluate the control.
3. Record the evidence request in the evidence tracking matrix.
4. Document the evidence owner and receipt date.
5. Review the evidence for relevance, completeness, and consistency.
6. Record findings and identify any follow-up requirements.

## Evidence Review Status

- Pending: Evidence has been requested but not yet reviewed.
- Reviewed: Evidence has been examined.
- Insufficient: Evidence does not adequately support the assessment conclusion.
- Not Provided: Requested evidence has not been received.

## Evidence Quality

Evidence should be relevant to the control, appropriate to the assessment period, and sufficient to support the conclusion.

Where evidence is incomplete or unavailable, document the limitation and request additional information when appropriate.

## Confidentiality

This portfolio uses fictional evidence descriptions and simulated assessment records. No confidential organizational information or real customer data should be uploaded.

## Limitation

This guide supports a simulated portfolio assessment and does not establish compliance or certification.
