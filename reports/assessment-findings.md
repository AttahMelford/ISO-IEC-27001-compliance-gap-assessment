# ISO 27001:2022 Assessment Findings

## 1. Purpose

This report will document the findings from the simulated ISO 27001:2022 compliance gap assessment of Meridian Digital Services.

## 2. Assessment Results

The assessment will evaluate 30 selected Annex A controls across organizational, people, physical, and technological security areas.

Results will be documented after the control assessments have been completed.

## 3. Findings Summary

Each finding will include:

- Finding ID
- Related ISO 27001 control
- Description of the identified gap
- Supporting evidence or evidence deficiency
- Risk rating
- Recommended corrective action
- Responsible owner

## 4. Recommendations

Recommendations will be based on the assessment evidence and the potential impact of identified gaps.

Remediation priorities will be documented and tracked through the remediation plan.

## 5. Limitations

This is a simulated assessment involving a fictional organization. Findings and recommendations will be based on the documented project scenario and hypothetical evidence.

This report does not represent an actual independent audit or certification assessment.
