# Risk Scoring Methodology

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the method used to assess and prioritize cybersecurity risks for the fictional Meridian Digital Services organization.

The methodology supports the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 risk assessment and treatment process. This is a simulated portfolio project, not a real organizational assessment.

## 2. Likelihood Scale

| Score | Rating | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | Unlikely to occur under normal circumstances |
| 2 | Unlikely | Could occur, but is not expected |
| 3 | Possible | Could reasonably occur |
| 4 | Likely | Expected to occur in many circumstances |
| 5 | Almost Certain | Expected to occur frequently or imminently |

## 3. Impact Scale

| Score | Rating | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Insignificant | Minimal disruption or loss |
| 2 | Minor | Limited disruption with low recovery effort |
| 3 | Moderate | Noticeable operational, financial, or compliance consequences |
| 4 | Major | Significant disruption, financial loss, or regulatory consequences |
| 5 | Severe | Critical business disruption, major data compromise, or severe legal or reputational consequences |

## 4. Risk Calculation

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

Both likelihood and impact are scored from 1 to 5. The resulting score ranges from 1 to 25.

| Score | Risk Level | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low | Routine monitoring and management |
| 5–9 | Moderate | Plan appropriate risk treatment |
| 10–16 | High | Prioritize risk treatment and management oversight |
| 17–25 | Critical | Escalate promptly for management attention and treatment decisions |

## 5. Inherent and Residual Risk

- **Inherent risk:** The estimated risk before considering the effect of existing controls.
- **Residual risk:** The estimated risk remaining after considering existing controls.

Residual risk scores must be supported by the effectiveness of existing controls. Planned or proposed controls must not be treated as implemented controls.

Both scores use the same likelihood × impact calculation.

## 6. Risk Treatment Options

- **Mitigate:** Implement or improve controls to reduce risk.
- **Accept:** Formally accept the risk through an authorized decision.
- **Avoid:** Stop or change the activity creating the risk.
- **Transfer:** Shift some financial or contractual consequences to another party, where appropriate.

Risk acceptance must be documented and approved by an authorized risk owner or management representative.

## 7. Assessment Limitations

All risks and scores in this portfolio are illustrative. They demonstrate a structured risk assessment approach and must not be interpreted as verified findings about a real organization.
