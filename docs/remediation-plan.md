# Remediation Plan

## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the corrective actions proposed to address gaps identified during the simulated ISO 27001:2022 compliance assessment.

## 2. Remediation Process

1. Document each identified gap.
2. Assess its risk and determine remediation priority.
3. Assign a responsible control owner.
4. Establish a target completion date.
5. Track progress and outstanding actions.
6. Validate supporting evidence before closing each finding.

## 3. Priority Levels

- High: Requires prompt attention due to potentially significant business or security impact.
- Medium: Requires planned corrective action to reduce identified risk.
- Low: Requires improvement or follow-up based on risk and business needs.

## 4. Tracking and Validation

Each remediation action will have an assigned owner, target date, status, and required closure evidence.

Actions will remain open until the corrective action has been completed and appropriate evidence has been reviewed.

## 5. Project Limitation

This plan is based on a simulated assessment of a fictional organization. Priorities and corrective actions are illustrative.
